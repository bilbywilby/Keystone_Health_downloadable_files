## Citation Hygiene Rules for This Project

1. **Never cite Tier B sources in regulator filings or letters without converting them to Tier A** — retrieve the primary document (statute text, regulation, agency publication) first.
2. **Reddit/community analyses (Catalog #13) are never citable** — context only.
3. **Every number in a published consumer document must trace to Tier A or an official agency publication.** Contributor-supplied figures stay [UNVERIFIED DATA] until upgraded.
4. **Conflict rule:** when two sources conflict (CMS phone; § 5533), the stricter/caution-oriented treatment wins in consumer-facing text until resolved.
5. **Update log:** append any new retrieval to the relevant Tier with date, and move resolved Tier C items up with their source.
