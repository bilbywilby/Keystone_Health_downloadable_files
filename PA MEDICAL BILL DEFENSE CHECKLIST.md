## ⚠️ FIRST — THREE THINGS THAT CAN HARM YOUR CASE

- [ ] **DO NOT IGNORE COURT PAPERS** — If you receive a summons, respond by the deadline. Contact legal aid immediately.
- [ ] **DO NOT MAKE PAYMENTS WHILE DISPUTING** — Even $5 or $10 can restart legal deadlines. Pay $0 until dispute is resolved.
- [ ] **DO NOT MISS INSURANCE APPEAL DEADLINES** — These are in your denial notices. Call 1-877-881-6388 (PA Insurance Dept) if unsure.

## STEP 1: TRIAGE — WHAT KIND OF BILL IS THIS? (5 MINUTES)

| Question | Answer | Action |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Did you sign a consent waiver at least 72 hours BEFORE service?** | ❌ No | You have strong protections → Go to Step 2 |
| | ✅ Yes | Your fight is harder → Go to Step 2 + read disclaimer |
| **2. Is the provider a nonprofit hospital?** | ✅ Yes | Ask for Financial Assistance Policy (FAP) → Also check below |
| | ❌ No | Standard dispute process → Go to Step 2 |
| **3. Did insurance pay at out-of-network rates?** | ✅ Yes | Possible No Surprises Act violation → Go to Step 2 |
| | ❌ No | Standard bill dispute → Go to Step 2 |

## STEP 2: GATHER YOUR PAPERS (DAY 1)

- [ ] **Itemized bill from provider** (not lump-sum statement)
- [ ] **Explanation of Benefits (EOB)** from your insurance
- [ ] **Insurance card** (front and back photo)
- [ ] **Any consent/waiver forms** you signed at registration
- [ ] **Calendar the dates:** Service date, first billing statement date

## STEP 3: SEND YOUR FIRST LETTER (DAYS 1–7)

Use certified mail with return receipt requested. Keep the green card!

- [ ] **Nonprofit hospital?** → Send FAP Demand Letter
- [ ] **Balance bill / out-of-network issue?** → Send Dispute Letter
- [ ] **Standard bill?** → Call insurer first to verify amount

**Template location:** Download from GitHub repo or ask clinic for copy

## 🚨 RED FLAGS — WHEN TO GET LAWYER HELP NOW

Call legal aid if ANY of these happen:

- [ ] You receive **court papers** (summons or complaint)
- [ ] Collector threatens **wage garnishment** (PA generally bans this for medical debt)
- [ ] Hospital reports to **credit bureau** before 120 days passed (if nonprofit)
- [ ] Bill is **over $5,000** or you fear a lawsuit
- [ ] You don't speak English fluently and need interpretation help

**PA Legal Aid Directory:** palegalfund.org

## 💰 KNOW YOUR EXEMPTIONS (IF SUED)

Pennsylvania protects some assets from collection:

| Protected | Details |
|---|---|
| **Wages** | Cannot garnish wages for ordinary medical debt (42 Pa.C.S. § 8127) |
| **Home (Tenancy by Entireties)** | Jointly-owned home shielded from single-spouse debt |
| **Bank Accounts** | ~$300 baseline exemption; Social Security/pensions often protected |
| **Personal Property** | Household goods, clothing, tools of trade generally safe |

*Note: These do NOT apply if you voluntarily pay a disputed bill. Consult counsel.*

## ⏰ KEY TIMELINES TO WATCH

| Timeline | What It Means | Deadline |
|---|---|---|
| **120-Day ECA Window** | Nonprofit hospital can't take "extraordinary" collection action | From first billing statement |
| **240-Day FAP Window** | Nonprofit must accept financial assistance application | From first billing statement |
| **Insurance Appeal Deadline** | Varies by plan — check your denial notice | Usually 180 days from denial |
| **Statute of Limitations** | Provider has 4 years to sue on written contract | From date of default |
| **Court Response Deadline** | If sued — act immediately | Varies by county (ask counsel) |

## ✅ POST-RESOLUTION STEPS

When bill is settled or dismissed:

- [ ] Get confirmation in **WRITING** (not just verbal)
- [ ] Check credit report at annualcreditreport.com (all 3 bureaus)
- [ ] Confirm collection account deleted (not just "paid")
- [ ] Keep all letters and certified mail receipts forever
- [ ] Delete digital files only after paper copies verified
