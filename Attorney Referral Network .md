# Attorney Referral Network — Operating Framework

## Purpose

Provide toolkit users with a structured pathway to licensed Pennsylvania
consumer-protection attorneys when their dispute exceeds the scope of
self-help.

## Referral Tiers

### Tier 1 — Free / Low-Cost (Disputes under $5,000)

| Organization | Contact | Scope |
|---|---|---|
| Pennsylvania Legal Aid Network | palawhelp.org/referral | General consumer, sliding scale |
| Community Legal Services of Philadelphia | clsphila.org/intake | Consumer protection, billing |
| Philadelphia Bar Association Lawyer Referral | pabar.org/lawyer-referral | General referral, 15-min free consult |
| Pennsylvania Ombudsman (insurance disputes) | pa.gov/ombudsman | Insurance-specific disputes |

### Tier 2 — Sliding Scale / Contingency ($5,000–$25,000)

- Pennsylvania Bar Association certified consumer law specialists
- Local legal aid societies outside Philadelphia (Pittsburgh, Allentown,
  Harrisburg, Erie)
- Law school clinic referrals (UPenn Carey, Temple X, Pitt)

### Tier 3 — Full Representation (Lawsuits, garnishment, multi-party)

- PA Bar Association specialist directory (consumer protection,
  health law)
- National Health Law Program (healthlaw.org) for systemic issues
- Class-action or multi-plaintiff coordination if pattern identified

## Intake Protocol

When a user's situation triggers an attorney referral:

1. **Assess urgency** (garnishment / lawsuit = same-day; balance bill
   under dispute = 1–2 weeks)
2. **Provide the matching tier** from the table above
3. **Supply a one-page summary** the user can hand to the attorney:
   - Provider name, NPI, date of service
   - Amount disputed
   - Statutes cited in the toolkit letter
   - Copies of all correspondence sent
   - Insurance EOB / explanation of benefits
4. **Track referral** (anonymized) for quarterly review metrics

## Maintainer Obligations

- Verify each referral contact annually (phone, website, active license)
- Remove any organization that fails verification
- Do NOT endorse individual attorneys by name (avoid UPL appearance)
- Do NOT negotiate fees on behalf of users
- Do NOT provide legal advice during the referral process — only
  logistics ("here is the number, here is what to bring")

## Data Handling

- Referral tracking is anonymized (no names, no amounts tied to
  identity)
- Aggregate counts only: "N referrals to Tier 1 in Q[X]"
- No user data shared with attorneys without explicit consent

## Review Cadence

- Annual full verification of all listed contacts
- Quarterly check that no listed organization has closed, changed
  scope, or been disciplined
- Immediate removal if a user reports a non-responsive or
  inappropriate referral (within 48 hours)
