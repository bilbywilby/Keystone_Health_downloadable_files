Dear Billing Compliance Officer,

Please be advised that the undersigned patient hereby disputes the above-referenced 
billing account(s) and demands the immediate cessation of all Extraordinary 
Collection Actions (ECAs), including credit bureau reporting, legal collection 
proceedings, and third-party placement, pursuant to Section 501(r) of the Internal 
Revenue Code (26 U.S.C. § 501(r)) and applicable Treasury Regulations.

As a tax-exempt 501(c)(3) hospital organization, [HOSPITAL NAME] is legally 
prohibited under 26 U.S.C. § 501(r)(6) from engaging in ECAs against an individual 
to collect a bill for care without first making reasonable efforts to determine 
whether the individual is eligible for assistance under the hospital's Financial 
Assistance Policy (FAP). Specifically, federal regulations mandate:

1. THE 120-DAY NOTICE WINDOW: A tax-exempt hospital may not initiate ECAs before 
   at least 120 days have passed from the date of the first post-discharge billing 
   statement, provided the facility has delivered adequate advance notice informing 
   the patient of the FAP.
2. THE 240-DAY APPLICATION WINDOW: If a patient submits an application for financial 
   assistance under the hospital's FAP within 240 days of the first post-discharge 
   billing statement, the hospital must suspend all ECAs while the application 
   is processed and a determination is rendered.
3. AMOUNTS GENERALLY BILLED (AGB) LIMITS: Pursuant to 26 U.S.C. § 501(r)(5), 
   emergency or other medically necessary care may not be charged to FAP-eligible 
   individuals at amounts exceeding the Amounts Generally Billed (AGB) to individuals 
   having insurance covering such care.
