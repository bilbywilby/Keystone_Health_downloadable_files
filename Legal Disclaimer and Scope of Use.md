# Legal Disclaimer & Scope of Use

## What This Toolkit Is

These templates provide **statutorily-grounded dispute frameworks** based on:

- Federal No Surprises Act (42 U.S.C. § 300gg-111; 45 C.F.R. Part 149)
- Pennsylvania Fair Credit Extension and Earned Wage Act (73 P.S. § 2270.1)
- Pennsylvania Unfair Trade Practices and Consumer Protection Law (73 P.S. § 201-2)
- CMS No Surprises Act interpretive guidance and FAQs

Each template cites specific regulatory provisions and is designed to
maximize regulatory leverage when disputing out-of-network balance bills
and related collections activity.

## What This Toolkit Is NOT

- **Not legal advice.** No attorney-client relationship is created by
  using these materials.
- **Not a guarantee of outcome.** No template, letter, or filing
  guarantees a specific result. Outcomes depend on facts, jurisdiction,
  timing, and the discretion of the adjudicating body.
- **Not a substitute for licensed counsel.** For significant disputes,
  consult a Pennsylvania-licensed attorney.

## When You Should Consult an Attorney

Use this toolkit as a first-line response. Escalate to an attorney if:

| Situation | Action |
|-----------|--------|
| Disputed amount exceeds $5,000 | Consult before sending |
| A lawsuit has been filed against you | Immediate consultation |
| Wage garnishment or bank levy initiated | Emergency consultation |
| Credit reporting damage has already occurred | Credit-specialist attorney |
| Multiple providers or insurers are involved | Consult before filing |
| The provider disputes the statutory basis | Consult before responding further |
