Complete Dispute Package Checklist & Exhibits
1. Exhibit A: The Primary Billing Statement & Itemized Invoice
What it is: The official statement, balance notice, or itemized bill received from the provider or hospital billing department.
Why it’s needed: Establishes the exact date of service, provider NPI/TIN, specific CPT/HCPCS codes billed, and the gross financial amount demanded.
2. Exhibit B: Insurance Documentation or Good Faith Estimate (GFE)
For Insured / Out-of-Network Claims: Include the full Explanation of Benefits (EOB) or denial notice from your health plan, showing what the insurer allowed, what was applied to your deductible/coinsurance, and any balance billing notices.
For Uninsured / Self-Pay Claims: Include the original Good Faith Estimate (GFE) provided by the convening provider prior to scheduling the service. Under federal rules (45 CFR § 149.620), you are eligible for dispute resolution if the final billed charges exceed the GFE by $400 or more.
3. Exhibit C: The FMV & MPFS Benchmark Calculation Sheet
What it is: The detailed mathematical reference sheet developed in the previous step, breaking down National RVUs, Pennsylvania GPCI regional multipliers, the 2026 Medicare Conversion Factor ($33.4009), and commercial scaling factors.
Why it’s needed: Replaces subjective chargemaster pricing with an objective, verifiable economic benchmark.
4. Exhibit D: Clinical Notes or Encounter Summaries (If Applicable)
What it is: Relevant extracts from your medical records, discharge summary, or operative notes that describe the actual intensity, time, and medical decision-making involved.
Why it’s needed: Proves that the service billed matched the coding criteria (e.g., verifying that a 99214 visit genuinely met the moderate medical decision-making or time threshold).
5. Exhibit E: Proof of Prior Communication / Good Faith Attempt to Resolve
What it is: Copies of prior emails, certified mail tracking receipts, or portal message transcripts showing you attempted to contact the provider's billing department or patient financial services to request an itemized review.
Suggested Assembly Order for Your Dispute Packet
Cover Letter / Formal Dispute Notice (Summarizing your request and pointing to the exhibits)
Exhibit A: Itemized Billing Statement
Exhibit B: Insurance EOB or Original GFE
Exhibit C: FMV Calculation Reference Sheet
Exhibit D: Clinical / Encounter Documentation (if required for coding validation)
Exhibit E: Postal or Electronic Communication History
