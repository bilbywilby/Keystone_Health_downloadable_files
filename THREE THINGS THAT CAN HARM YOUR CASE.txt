### Quick Screen: Is Your Plan Fully Insured or Self-Funded?

**Likely Fully Insured if:**
- Your insurance card shows a major carrier (BCBS, Aetna, Cigna, Highmark, etc.)
- Your employer has fewer than 100 employees
- Your denial notice mentions PID external review or Act 146 rights

**Likely Self-Funded if:**
- Your employer is large (100+ employees) or a government entity
- Your EOB says "self-funded," "ERISA," or "administratively serviced"
- Your denial notice only mentions federal IDR or CMS complaint routes

**When in doubt:** Call your HR/benefits department and ask: 
"Is my health plan fully insured (state-regulated) or self-funded (ERISA)?"
Get the answer in writing if possible.
