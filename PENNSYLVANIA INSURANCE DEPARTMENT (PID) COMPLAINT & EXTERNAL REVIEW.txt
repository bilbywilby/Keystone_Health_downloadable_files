III. STATEMENT OF COMPLAINT & STATUTORY GROUNDS FOR REGULATORY RELIEF
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The undersigned policyholder hereby files this formal complaint with the Pennsylvania 
Insurance Department requesting regulatory investigation, enforcement, and an 
independent external review determination regarding illegal balance billing and/or 
improper denial of coverage.

This complaint is grounded on the following state and federal statutory protections:

A. NO SURPRISES ACT & 45 C.F.R. PART 149:
   The disputed service constitutes [ emergency care / an out-of-network ancillary 
   service performed at an in-network facility / air ambulance transport ]. Under 
   45 C.F.R. § 149.110 and § 149.120, out-of-network providers are prohibited from 
   balance billing covered enrollees beyond in-network cost-sharing requirements 
   (Qualifying Payment Amount - QPA). The carrier and provider failed to properly 
   adjudicate this claim in accordance with federal and state surprise billing standards.

B. PENNSYLVANIA ACT 146 OF 2022 (40 P.S. § 991.2161 ET SEQ.):
   Pursuant to Act 146 (SB 225), Pennsylvania enrollees maintain the statutory 
   right to initiate an independent external review of adverse benefit determinations 
   and coverage denials. The insurer's final internal appeal determination failed 
   to adhere to statutory clinical necessity, prior authorization, or emergency 
   coverage mandates.

C. PA FAIR CREDIT EXTENSION UNIFORMITY ACT (FCEUA - 73 P.S. § 2270.1 ET SEQ.):
   The provider and/or its collection agency have engaged in unfair and deceptive 
   collection practices pursuant to 73 P.S. § 2270.4 by pursuing collection 
   communications and threatening credit bureau reporting on an account subject 
   to an active, formal statutory dispute.
