🏛️ Pennsylvania & Federal Medical Debt Legal Framework

This interactive statutory reference module outlines the statutory citations, procedural defenses, and regulatory complaint routes used by the Keystone CMS. Toggle counsel verification flags to review legal authority statuses before executing consumer disputes.

Pennsylvania Contract Law

42 Pa.C.S. § 5525(a) — Statute of Limitations

VERIFIED

Establishes a 4-year statute of limitations on debt collection actions originating from express or implied contracts. The clock accrues from the date of default or missed payment statement.

⚠️ The "No-Payment" Rule & Acknowledgment Doctrine:

Under PA common law (Huntingdon Finance Corp. v. Newtown Artesian Water Co., 442 Pa. Super. 406), making a partial payment or executing a written acknowledgment may reset the 4-year clock. Time-barred accounts must be issued SOL Termination notices with strict instructions to pay $0.

Federal Tax Law

26 U.S.C. § 501(r)(6) & Treas. Reg. § 1.501(r)-6

VERIFIED

Requires non-profit 501(c)(3) hospitals to refrain from Extraordinary Collection Actions (ECAs) for at least 120 days from the first post-discharge bill, and to process Financial Assistance Policy (FAP) applications for 240 days.

Enforcement Route:

Violations require filing IRS Form 13909 and PA Attorney General Bureau of Consumer Protection complaints alleging unfair/deceptive practices under 73 P.S. § 2270.5(a).

Federal Healthcare Act

No Surprises Act — 45 C.F.R. Part 149

VERIFIED

Protects consumers from balance billing for emergency care, air ambulance services, and out-of-network ancillary services performed at in-network facilities.

Resolution Protocol:

Initiates a 30-business-day Open Negotiation window (45 CFR § 149.510). Unresolved disputes escalate to CMS Federal IDR or Pennsylvania Insurance Department (PID) review under Act 146.

PA Debt & Wage Protections

73 P.S. § 2270.1 (FCEUA) & 42 Pa.C.S. § 8127

COUNSEL REVIEW

FCEUA governs unfair debt collection practices in PA. PA law heavily restricts wage garnishment under 42 Pa.C.S. § 8127(a), prohibiting wage attachment for medical debt.

Statute of Limitations on FCEUA:

Subject to PA's 6-year catch-all SOL (42 Pa.C.S. § 5527(b)). Pending PA HB 1731 proposes a reduced 3-year limit for consumer debt litigation.
